6-STEP CARE ROADMAP

Your Fertility Journey, Step by Step

Understanding what happens at each stage relieves uncertainty. Here is our structured pathway from consultation to parenthood.

01

Initial Consultation & Assessment

Comprehensive fertility evaluation including hormonal tests, ultrasound scan analysis, and joint medical history review. Dr. Aishwarya personally designs your plan.

02

Ovarian Stimulation & Monitoring

Controlled medication protocol to stimulate your ovaries to produce multiple eggs. Regular 4D ultrasound and blood hormone monitoring track precise follicle growth.

03

Gentle Egg Retrieval

A minimally invasive ultrasound-guided procedure performed under light sedation. Safe, comfortable, and takes approximately 15–20 minutes with swift recovery.

04

Fertilisation & Embryo Culture

Eggs are fertilized with healthy sperm in our Class 10,000 embryology cleanroom. Embryos are nurtured to Day 5 blastocyst stage under strict climate control.

05

Embryo Transfer

The highest-quality embryo is gently placed into the uterus in a painless procedure. Surplus viable embryos are vitrified for future family planning.

06

Pregnancy Test & Follow Up

A blood beta-hCG test confirms pregnancy 14 days after transfer. Our dedicated medical team provides continuous first-trimester monitoring and supportive care.

Every treatment plan is personally directed by Dr. Aishwarya Parthasarathy.

No junior proxies — you meet the lead specialist at every pivotal milestone.
